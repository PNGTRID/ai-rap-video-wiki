# Best-Friends AI Rap Video Prompt

Intent: friends
Wiki: https://ai-rap-video.wiki/prompts/ai-rap-video-best-friends-duo/

## When to use

Two friends, hype energy: reunions, birthdays, fantasy-league wins, "we've been friends since" posts. Dials the core duo template up in energy and sideways in warmth — bigger gestures, synchronized moments, playful roast-and-toast bars.

## Hook & verse structure

Roast-and-toast: left opens with a light jab (about the situation, never appearance), right fires back, both land a togetherness line. Generate with the lyrics template at tone `playful`; one jab each plus one shared punchline.

## Main prompt

```text
Create an AI rap video duo performance with best-friends energy from two separate portrait references.
Left performer = photo 1, right performer = photo 2, fixed sides for the whole clip.
Stage: original booth-style set, warm orange key light, one hanging microphone between them, dark backdrop with soft rim light.
Wardrobe: matching varsity-style jackets over each performer's own look, same color scheme on both.
Action: high-energy best-friend performance — traded bars with sharp mouth movement, synchronized head nods on the beat, one high-five on the beat drop, shoulders bumping once mid-clip, big grins throughout.
Choreography rule: the high-five happens exactly once, at the midpoint; hands meet in the center of frame and return to sides immediately.
Camera: locked medium two-shot, subtle push-in during the final two seconds.
Audio: original playful rap cadence, roast-and-toast call-and-response — write NEW original lyrics; never quote commercial songs.
Duration: 8-12 seconds, ending on a shared confident pose for the loop hold.
```

## Beat & camera notes

1. 0-2s — hype open, both nodding, left spits the jab.
2. 2-5s — counter lands with a shoulder bump.
3. 5-7s — one high-five on the drop, hands meet center frame, back to position.
4. 7-12s — shared toast line, matching pose hold (loop point).

## Negative prompt

```text
identity swap, merged faces, third person appearing, extra arms during high-five, deformed hands, extra fingers, blurry faces, aggressive expressions, weapons, alcohol, on-screen lyrics or captions, watermarks, brand logos, celebrity lookalike, NSFW, scene changes, camera shake
```

## Photo prep

Photos where each friend is already smiling; similar heights read best; crop to include arms so the high-five has hands to work with.

## Render steps (any model)

1. Slot-order uploads, paste prompt, add the roast-and-toast script to the Audio block.
2. If the high-five produces extra arms, cut it (keep shoulder bumps) before touching anything else.
3. Check the midpoint frame: one clean clap, four hands total; ending pose matches the opening for the loop.

## Failure modes

- Extra arms during high-five → enforce "exactly once, at the midpoint" or drop to shoulder bumps.
- Reads aggressive → soften the jab lines, pick smiling inputs.
- Jackets invented differently → name the color in "matching varsity-style jackets".

## Compliance

Original staging and lyrics only. Do not commercially bind protected recordings (including the Hotel Lobby trend audio) or celebrity likenesses. Both friends must consent to the render and the post.
