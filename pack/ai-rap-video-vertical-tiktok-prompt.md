# Vertical AI Rap Video Prompt for TikTok

Intent: 9:16 social
Wiki: https://ai-rap-video.wiki/prompts/ai-rap-video-vertical-tiktok-prompt/

## When to use

The clip's home is TikTok, Reels, or Shorts. Portrait framing, UI-safe heads, hook-first structure, and a seamless loop — all baked into the prompt.

## Hook & verse structure

Inverted for the feed: hook first (two most quotable lines in the first two seconds), verse after. Left performer opens, right lands the punchline. Name the occasion in the hook.

## Main prompt

```text
Create a vertical 9:16 AI rap video duo performance from two separate portrait references.
Left performer = photo 1, right performer = photo 2, fixed sides for the entire clip.
Aspect and framing: strict vertical 9:16, both performers framed from the chest up, heads in the middle third of the frame — leave the top 15% and bottom 20% clear of faces for platform UI and captions.
Stage: original booth-style set, warm orange key light, one hanging microphone centered between the performers, dark backdrop with a soft vertical light strip.
Action: the clip opens mid-energy — both performers already nodding on beat; left performer delivers the opening hook line with sharp mouth movement, right performer lands the answer line, then they trade short bars.
Camera: static vertical framing with one subtle push-in at the midpoint; no pans, no rotation.
Audio: original upbeat rap cadence, hook-first structure, call-and-response roles — write NEW original lyrics; never quote commercial songs.
Duration: 8-11 seconds; the final two-shot hold matches the opening framing so the clip loops seamlessly.
```

## Beat & camera notes

1. 0-2s — hook cold open, mid-line on frame one.
2. 2-6s — traded verse inside the middle band.
3. 6-9s — shared lean into the mic (clip-able moment).
4. 9-11s — hold mirrors frame one for an invisible loop.

## Negative prompt

```text
horizontal letterboxing, black bars, 16:9 crop, cropped foreheads or chins, faces outside safe area, identity swap, merged faces, third person, extra limbs, blurry faces, on-screen lyrics or captions burned in, watermarks, brand logos, celebrity lookalike, NSFW, camera rotation, scene changes
```

## Photo prep

Crop with headroom; similar subject distance across photos; plain backdrops survive 9:16 best.

## Render steps (any model)

1. Set 9:16 in the tool settings before prompting.
2. Slot-order uploads, paste prompt, negative block in its field or after "Avoid:".
3. Render 8-11s — shorter loops replay more.
4. Compare first and last frame; if they don't match, reinforce "final hold matches the opening framing".

## Failure modes

- Heads cropped by UI → restate safe-area bands, framing chest-up.
- Render returns 16:9 → fix the tool's aspect setting, not the prompt.
- Visible loop jump → strengthen the mirror-frame-one clause.

## Compliance

Original staging and lyrics only. Do not commercially bind protected recordings (including the Hotel Lobby trend audio) or celebrity likenesses. Consent from everyone in the photos.
