## The meta-lesson: time-to-first-clip

Seventeen minutes is honest. Anyone promising a one-click AI rap video is selling a credit queue. The realistic budget for a first render is twenty minutes of prep plus model time, and — this is the part tutorials rarely say — **re-renders get much faster** because photos, prompt structure, and negative blocks are already done. That compounding is the whole argument for keeping your workflow in plain text files instead of a locked generator.
