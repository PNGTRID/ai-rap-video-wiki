"Free" is the most overloaded word in the AI video space. This **step-by-step free-pipeline tutorial** from WealthWise is a useful honesty check: it builds an AI rap music video without paid tools, and in doing so shows exactly what free costs — mostly patience and re-renders. This page embeds the video and separates the genuinely free parts of the workflow from the parts where sites quietly want your card.

## What this video covers

A no-budget walkthrough at this length chains free tiers together: a generation tool on a free plan, free editing, free posting. The through-line worth noticing is that the *creative* inputs — photos, prompt, lyrics — cost nothing anywhere. What free tiers ration is attempts. That is why the free path rewards preparation: every wasted generation is a longer wait.

## How to make the free path actually work

1. **Prep like it costs money.** Front-lit photos, one face each, distinct outfits — the [checklist](/prompts/ai-rap-video-photo-prep-checklist/) exists because free queues punish retry loops.
2. **Write the negative block first.** Every failure the [library](/prompts/ai-rap-video-negative-prompt-guide/) prevents is a queue slot saved.
3. **Fix with wording, not re-rolls.** The [troubleshooting guide](/prompts/ai-rap-video-troubleshooting-faces/) maps each symptom to one line change. A targeted fix beats three blind regenerations on any tier.
4. **Keep audio original.** A free clip bound to a protected track is a free takedown; [original bars](/prompts/ai-rap-video-original-lyrics-prompt/) are also free.
